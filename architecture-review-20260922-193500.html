Candidate 01: Deepen the Logistics & Transport Dispatch module

BaselineDispatchPolicy is the clearest example of shallowness in the codebase: its interface leaks internal route occupancies, topology distance functions, and downstream node acceptance lambdas across the seam, while EpisodeEngine retains all actual state management. Deepening Logistics collapses over 350 lines of scattered fleet and reservation logic into one cohesive module with an interface that tests can exercise without writing callback fixtures.
